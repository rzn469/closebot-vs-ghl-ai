## What GHL users actually mean by "best chatbot"

The evaluation bar for a GHL chatbot is narrow and practical:

- Does it reply in seconds and keep replying, or does it go quiet after two messages?
- Can it qualify (budget, service type, location, timeline) and write that into custom fields?
- Can it book, reschedule, and cancel without a human patching webhooks at 9pm?
- What does it cost per conversation, and what does the agency pay versus re-bill?

Nobody searching this term cares about "AI-powered transformation." They care whether the bot books or embarrasses them. That's the lens everything below uses.

## The bill that isn't on the pricing table

Two costs sit outside the plan price, and both surprise people.

The first is the CRM underneath. CloseBot rides on top of HighLevel, HubSpot, or a custom CRM — it doesn't replace one. GoHighLevel's own plans start at $97/month for Starter and $297/month for Unlimited, so a business running 1,000 CloseBot replies a month is realistically at $84 plus a CRM subscription, roughly $181/month before any telephony or WhatsApp fees.

The second is AI provider tokens. CloseBot's documentation states plainly that message pricing doesn't cover the token costs charged by your AI provider, and that V2 expects you to bring your own API keys. Awkwardly, the FAQ on the plans page still says bring-your-own-key isn't allowed for security compliance reasons. Those two statements contradict each other on the vendor's own site, and the difference is a real monthly number, so confirm it with support before you commit to a volume tier rather than trusting either page.

For comparison, the GHL-native route bundles tokens into its own model pricing, which is simpler to forecast and harder to control.

## What to watch out for

No refunds is a real constraint, not a footnote. The 7-day trial is where you do your testing, and the Free plan lets you keep testing indefinitely at low volume.

The agent is only as good as what you feed it. A G2 reviewer's line that stuck with me: if the pipeline, messaging, offer, or follow-up logic is sloppy, the AI scales that sloppiness faster. One independent review scores setup effort at 3 out of 5 and estimates 5 to 10 hours of initial configuration for knowledge bases and connections. Budget for that, or budget for a certified builder.

Also worth stating clearly: CloseBot is text-only. No voice agent, no phone calls. If your lead flow is mostly inbound calls, this isn't the tool — GHL's own Voice AI or a voice-specific platform is.

And it's CRM-native, not channel-native. CloseBot has no standalone Instagram or WhatsApp connection. It answers the channels connected to your CRM inbox, which works fine if Instagram DMs are already landing in HighLevel's Conversations tab, and doesn't work at all if your entire pipeline lives in Instagram and you don't run a CRM.

## What setup actually looks like

The build is boring in the good way, based on CloseBot's own documentation:

1. In the app, go to Sources, add a new source, and pick HighLevel Sub-Account. You connect through an OAuth popup — no API key copy-pasting.
2. Build a Job Flow: the objective, the qualifying questions, the calendar it books to, and the custom fields it writes back to GHL.
3. Give the agent knowledge — documents, URLs, and FAQs. This is the part that determines whether it sounds competent or generic.
4. Test in the Testing Portal against a real sub-account, then go live.
5. Watch Smart FAQ for questions the agent dodged, answer them once, and let CloseBot re-engage the leads who asked.

Rollback is available, and you can pause the AI on any individual conversation for a human takeover, which is the feature that makes teams comfortable leaving it on overnight.

Two things, technically. There's a free-forever plan capped at 100 messages a month, and a 7-day trial on any paid plan before the first charge. There are no refunds after that.

**How much does it cost per month?**

**Does CloseBot work with GoHighLevel out of the box?**
Yes. There's a native HighLevel integration with contact tagging, custom field mapping, and workflow triggers, connected through OAuth on the Sources page.

**Can it handle Instagram or WhatsApp DMs?**
Only through your CRM. If those channels are wired into your GHL Conversations inbox, the agent answers them. If they aren't, CloseBot has no direct connection of its own.

**Is it better than GHL's native Conversation AI?**
For objective-driven qualification and booking, the community and third-party consensus leans yes, and the pricing flexibility for agencies is clearly better. For cheap FAQ handling on a handful of sub-accounts, native GHL is simpler and sometimes cheaper. Judge it by whether booking is the point of the bot.
